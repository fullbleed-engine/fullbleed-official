What we count.

Households are deduplicated within the annual program register. Volunteer hours include recorded event and maintenance time. Space counts refer to year-round locations, not individual events.

Quarterly counts show participation within each period and must not be added to estimate unique annual reach.

What comes next.

Extend evening access. Improve step-free entrances. Publish a simple monthly view of program activity and spending.
